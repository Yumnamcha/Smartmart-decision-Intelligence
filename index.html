Task 5 — Decision Making
Evidence-based decisions with data support, justification, and quantified outcomes
1
Impose a 15% discount ceiling on Furniture across all regions
CATEGORY: Pricing Policy · URGENCY: Immediate · IMPACT: High
Critical
What Decision?

Cap all Furniture product discounts at 15%. Require VP-level approval for any exception above 12%.

Data Support

Furniture avg discount = 37%. Net loss = -$105K. Every 10% excess discount generates negative margin. 8 of top 10 loss products are Furniture.

Why Correct?

Furniture has high fixed costs (warehousing, last-mile delivery). Volume cannot compensate. The losses are structural, not cyclical.

Expected Outcome

Reduce Furniture losses by $60–70K within 2 quarters. Net margin improves from -7.2% to 8–12%. Some volume decline is acceptable.

2
Increase Technology marketing budget by 20% in North & East
CATEGORY: Investment · URGENCY: This Quarter · IMPACT: High
Recommended
What Decision?

Reallocate 20% of total marketing budget to Technology in the two highest-performing regions.

Data Support

Technology margin = 28.4%. North generates $142K from Technology alone. ML forecast shows +22% organic growth. No discount support needed.

Why Correct?

Invest where margins are high and demand is growing organically. ROI on marketing spend is highest in proven, high-margin categories.

Expected Outcome

Additional $80–100K profit within 2 quarters. North Region targets $400K+ annual profit. Technology becomes $350K+ category by end-2025.

3
Place South Region under Profit Recovery Plan — freeze new discounts above 20%
CATEGORY: Regional Turnaround · URGENCY: Critical · IMPACT: Very High
Urgent
What Decision?

Immediately freeze all new discount authorisations above 20% in South. Assign a dedicated turnaround team.

Data Support

South = -$109K net loss. Discount rate 34.2%. All 5 categories unprofitable. Isolation Forest flagged 28 anomalous orders in South alone.

Why Correct?

A region cannot sustain operating losses indefinitely. Discount freeze stops incremental margin destruction while structural fixes are planned.

Expected Outcome

Within 60 days: losses fall below -$40K. Within 6 months: break-even achievable. Loyalty programme as discount alternative to retain customers.
